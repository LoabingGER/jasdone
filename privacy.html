Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. What data we receive

When you log in with TikTok, the app receives only what the permissions you approve allow:

  • user.info.basic – your TikTok account ID, display name and profile picture, used to show which account is connected.
  • video.publish – permission to publish videos to your account. To follow TikTok's rules, the app also reads your posting options (for example which privacy levels are available) before each upload.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, so the app can upload without you logging in each time.

The app does not read your messages, followers, likes, comments, contacts or location.

2. How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish videos to your account. Access tokens are stored on the app operator's own server and are not sent anywhere except to TikTok. We do not sell, share or use the data for advertising or analytics.

3. Videos

Videos and captions are created on the operator's server and sent to TikTok when they are published. Once published, they are handled by TikTok under the TikTok Privacy Policy.

4. How to remove your data

You can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions). After that the stored tokens no longer work. You can also ask us to delete them (see below); we will do so within 30 days.

5. Children

The app is not intended for people under the minimum age required to use TikTok.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available on this page.
